EX\_16JUN - Student Study Tracker

You're building a simple tool to track how many minutes different students spent studying each day.

Tasks:

&#x20;- Print a numbered list of all entries (use enumerate()) in the format:

&#x09;1. Alice studied Maths for 45 minutes

&#x20;- Calculate total minutes studied per student (use a dictionary)

&#x20;- Use a list comprehension to get a list of students who studied more than 40 minutes in a single session (just the names, duplicates are fine)

&#x20;- Find the student with the most total study time

&#x20;- Bonus: Ask the user to enter a minimum total minutes threshold, and print only students who meet or exceed it
