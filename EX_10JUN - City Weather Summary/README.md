EX\_10JUN - City Weather Summary

Have a list of cities with their recorded temperatures for the week (in °C).

Tasks:

&#x20;- For each city, calculate and print the average temperature (rounded to 1 decimal).

&#x20;- Use a list comprehension to get the names of cities where at least one day was below 5°C.

&#x20;- Print the city with the highest average temperature.
